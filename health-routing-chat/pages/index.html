Barbados Health Services

If you have an call 511 or go to QEH Accident & Emergency immediately.

The following situations require you to call 511 or go directly to A&E.

Resuscitation — call 511 now

  • Cardiac arrest or not breathing
  • Unresponsive / unconscious
  • Severe difficulty breathing or choking
  • Massive uncontrolled bleeding
  • Suspected stroke — face drooping, arm weakness, slurred speech

Emergent — go to A&E immediately

  • Chest pain or pressure
  • Severe allergic reaction (swelling, hives, throat tightening)
  • Seizure or convulsions
  • High fever with stiff neck or rash
  • Suspected poisoning or overdose
  • Severe head, neck or spinal injury
  • Severe burns covering a large area
  • Sudden severe headache unlike any before

Not sure? Call 511 — the operator will advise you.
